THE HARDEST FREE PROGRAM you'll ever take

What this is

Our bootcamp is the gate into Frost. It's how we pick talent. It's a seven-day program of real design work that tells us more about a designer than any interview could.

We teach you the rigor of how design is actually done at production scale. No gatekeeping, no trend-chasing. These are fundamentals that last a career.

All programs follow the same structure: a lesson with a daily assignment; we go through everyone's work and give direct critique; you revise and apply the feedback. We repeat until the program ends.

The true value of this program is the daily critique most designers never get from anyone at this level.

Your Instructor

The bootcamp program is taught by John Paul de Guzman, Frost's founder and Chief Creative Officer

John Paul de Guzman is the founder and Chief Creative Officer of Frost. He's spent over a decade building design teams and shipping production work for some of the country's largest digital products, and started Frost to close the gap between what design schools teach and what the job actually demands.

He leads every bootcamp cohort personally — not because Frost can't scale, but because he believes the daily critique is the entire point. Outside of Frost, he mentors early-career designers and writes occasionally about design systems and creative leadership.
